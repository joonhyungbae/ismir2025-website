# Timeline: what the site needed, and when

A conference website is not designed once. What visitors need changes with the phases of the conference, and the site has to change with them. This page records when pages and menu items appeared on the ISMIR 2025 website, reconstructed from the project's version and deployment history, so that the next web chair can plan the year.

ISMIR 2025 milestones: paper deadline March 28, 2025; acceptance notifications June 6, 2025; early-bird registration deadline August 4, 2025; conference September 21 to 25, 2025 (satellite events September 20 and 26).

Between September 1 and 20, 11 pages were added and five older ones were merged into others. The code nearly doubled in those three weeks: 47% of the final code was written then, and the public site was deployed 84 times.

Menus for the program and for attending existed in the code from November 2024 but were kept hidden (commented out) until there was content behind them. The attendance menu appeared on June 3, just before notifications, and absorbed registration. The program menu appeared on June 7 and moved to first place on June 12.

The menu never changed again after September 18. A year later the virtual platform link still sits right after the program.

## Recommendations

- **Decide the order of phases at the start, not their content.** Sketch what the menu should put first during the calls, registration, the program, and the conference, and what each phase will push down.
- **Leave room for what comes last.** Travel guides, social events, and the final program arrive in the last weeks whatever you ask for. Reserve their place in the menu early.
- **Watch the menu width.** With nine top-level items the ISMIR 2025 menu wrapped onto a second line on a 1,366-pixel laptop screen. Move items that have served their phase into dropdowns.
- **Plan your own year in two unequal parts:** about a day a week until notifications, most days after them.
- **Keep a preview step and a second reviewer,** above all in the final weeks.
- **Design the archive phase.** Before handing over, rearrange the menu once more: the program, the papers, and the awards, not the logistics of a week that has passed.
